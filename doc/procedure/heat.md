# Heat

![Heat decapsulation of a ceramic-packaged integrated circuit using a MAPP gas torch.](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/46/Heat_decap_with_MAPP_gas.jpg)

*Heat decapsulation of a ceramic-packaged integrated circuit using a [MAPP gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAPP_gas) torch.*

Heat can be used for decapping ceramic packages. I use a [MAPP gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAPP_gas) torch: more power than propane, safer than acetylene.
